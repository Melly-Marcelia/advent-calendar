# Video walkthrough script

A step-by-step outline for recording a "how to use it" video on the live site
(https://melly-marcelia.github.io/advent-calendar/). It takes about 3–5 minutes.

**Before recording:** open the site in a fresh browser profile or a private window so the screen
starts empty. Have 2–3 pictures ready to upload (photos, stickers, a Spotify code screenshot).
To show the theme uploader, also have a folder of door images named `day01-default.png`,
`day01-hover.png`, ….

---

### 1. Intro (home page)
- "This is an advent calendar you can make for someone: 24 doors, one surprise a day, no account needed."
- Point out the **Demo mode** banner at the bottom: on this site everything is saved in your own browser.

### 2. The example calendar
- Click **see the demo** (top right).
- Hover a few frames to show the hover artwork, then click one to open its surprise.
- Close it and go back to the home page.

### 3. Create your own
- Click **Create your calendar**. You're now on your dashboard.
- Show the **Share link** and **Edit link**:
  - The share link goes to the person the calendar is for.
  - The edit link is private and is how you get back in. Click **Copy** and save it somewhere.

### 4. Fill in a few days
- Day 1: type a message, choose a **Gift image**, then click **Save day 1**. Point out "Saved ✓".
- Day 2: add a message plus a **Gift code** image (e.g. a Spotify code).
- Edit two more days without saving. Point out the **Save all changed (2)** button and click it.
- Mention the counter: "N of 24 days have content".

### 5. Settings & theme
- Open **Calendar settings & theme**.
- Change the title (e.g. "For Sam 🎄") and subtitle, then click **Save settings**.
- Explain **Unlock mode**:
  - *Date-gated*: door 1 opens on 1 December, door 2 on the 2nd, and so on.
  - *Always unlocked*: every door opens right away (demo calendars start like this).
- Optional: **Upload a new theme**. Select the whole folder of `dayXX-default/hover` files and
  watch the door previews change.

### 6. See it as the recipient
- Click **Preview** next to the share link. A new tab opens with the calendar the recipient will see.
- Open the doors you filled in to show the message and pictures.

### 7. Coming back later
- Refresh the dashboard to show that everything is still there.
- Paste the edit link into the address bar to show how you get back in.

### 8. Wrap-up
- **Delete this calendar** removes everything (show the confirmation, then cancel or confirm).
- "On this demo site your calendar stays in your browser. The full version with a Supabase
  backend makes share links work on any device. The README explains how to set it up."
